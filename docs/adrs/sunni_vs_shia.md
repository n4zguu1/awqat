# Initial prayer-time scope

- **Status:** Accepted

## Context

Sunni and Shia communities may group and schedule prayers differently. Shia
users may combine Dhuhr with Asr and Maghrib with Isha.

## Options considered

- Support both conventions independently.
- Display only the Sunni prayer schedule initially.

## Decision

Display the Sunni prayer schedule in the initial version to reduce development
overhead. Supporting other conventions can be considered later.
