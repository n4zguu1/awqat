# Main screen

## Goal

Show the user's current local date and the most important prayer information
in one terminal screen. The first version prioritizes feature completeness
over visual polish.

## Acceptance criteria

- The selected city is visible.
- Gregorian and Hijri dates are visible.
- Today's prayer times are visible.
- The next prayer and remaining time are visible.
- The active calculation method and madhab are visible.
- Configuration can be opened from the screen.
- Calculation or configuration errors are shown explicitly.
